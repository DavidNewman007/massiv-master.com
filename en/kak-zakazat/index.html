How to order

Ordering, payment and shipping

  1. Choose a piece or material

    Add what you like to your selection, or press “Ask about buying” — Telegram will open with a message containing the item name and link.

  2. I confirm availability

    Every item is one of a kind, so first I check it is still available and, if you like, send extra photos and video, measurements and moisture readings.

  3. Payment

    We agree on the payment method in our chat. For made-to-order pieces there is a deposit, with the balance due on completion. Prices are in Russian roubles (₽).

  4. Delivery

    Pick-up in Sochi. Across Russia I ship by freight carrier and handle the packing and hand-over myself. Large slabs and tables by arrangement. For delivery outside Russia, message me and we will look at the options.
